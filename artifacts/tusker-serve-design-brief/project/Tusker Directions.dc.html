Where they differ, in decisions
Runtime truth. 1a keeps it out of the way — one health word in the nav, capacity in a “More” line. 1b gives it a permanent rail, which makes the machine legible but spends 320 px on it forever.
Grouping. 1a groups with hairlines and space, so a healthy project is almost entirely white. 1b groups with panels, so density stays even whether there are two items or twelve.
Attention. 1a uses a full-width band with a coloured top rule — one red region dominates naturally. 1b uses a left accent on a card, which scales to several items but reads quieter at a glance.
Risk. 1a is more distinctive and more fragile: long outcome titles and dense diagnostics tables will fight the editorial grid. 1b is safer for Settings and Diagnostics, and less memorable.
