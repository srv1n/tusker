Probe requirements

A runner must answer a version and capability probe before a claim is created. The probe is read-only: it may not dispatch work, mutate a workspace, or consume quota. Health is established before an attempt exists, so a missing executable can never produce a half-started run.

Invariant Runner failure happens before claim. A runner that cannot be probed is infrastructure_blocked, never active.

The probe compares the installed capability manifest against what the task contract needs — harness, model, effort level, permission mode, and subagent limits. A mismatch is reported as a capability defect with the exact missing feature, not as a generic failure. See Authority boundaries and 14 · Opt-in factory execution control.

Reporting alternatives

A failing probe may report other executables it discovered, with version and provenance. It may not substitute one. Selecting a discovered executable is an explicit local policy change, recorded as a receipt, because the authorized delivery was approved against a specific runner profile.
