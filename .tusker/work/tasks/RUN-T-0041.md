# RUN-T-0041 · Crashrecovery e2e suite leaks fixtures: fake-runner and wrapper processes orphan and accumulate forever

## Intent

Found today (2026-07-08) during a routine backpressure gate. 624 live `fake-runner --mode hold` processes — plus their `tusker runner-wrapper` parents — were on the machine, all under `/var/folders/.../tusker-crash-e2e-bin-*/` and `/tmp/tusker-crash-daemon-restart-dead-wrapper-*/` state dirs, i.e. fixtures spawned by the `e2e/crashrecovery` tests. Every `go test ./...` (the standard backpressure gate every runner attempt runs) leaks more; they never exit, accumulate without bound, and alarmed the operator ("numerous fake runners"). Cleared today by hand with `pkill`. The tests deliberately exercise wrapper adoption / crash recovery, so the fixtures are legitimate — but they never reap their own children.

Semantics:

1. **Reap on exit.** Every crashrecovery fixture that spawns a runner-wrapper or fake-runner reaps its children on test exit — a defer / `t.Cleanup` that kills the whole process group, not just the direct child, so adopted/re-parented processes die too.
2. **Self-expiring holds.** Hold-mode fake runners carry a hard wall-clock timeout (bounded well above the longest test) so any orphan that escapes a cleanup self-exits instead of living forever.
3. **Suite-end assertion.** A suite-teardown check asserts no fixture process survives (by state-dir prefix / marker) and fails the suite if the reap missed any — so a future leak is caught by the gate, not by the operator noticing hundreds of processes.
