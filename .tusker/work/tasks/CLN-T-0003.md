# CLN-T-0003 · Delete legacy V5/V6 surface, stubs, and stale docs

## Intent

Delete rather than fence. Remove the legacy V5/V6 surface that V7 only stubbed out: `removed_surfaces.go` and the ~40 `legacyOnlyCommand` dispatch cases, the `tusker legacy` command group, the demoted docs-publication material (`skill/references/DOCS_PUBLICATION.md`, the quarantined Diátaxis/publication sections of `docs/documentation-model.md`), stale legacy templates that teach retired vocabulary, and dead `research/` legacy bundles.
