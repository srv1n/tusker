## Intent
Stopping a landing gate or closeout check from the Processes section records
the operator's intent, but the gate sees an ordinary failed command and the
work can be retried as if a test had failed. A check stopped by the operator
must end as stopped by the operator and must not be retried automatically.

## Failure and edge cases
- A check that fails on its own still reports failed and follows today's retry
  rules.
- A check stopped for silence keeps its stuck, stopped outcome.
