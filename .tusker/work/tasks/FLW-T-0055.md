## Intent

Make a blocked approval discoverable and actionable in Tusker: click Approve, durably save the scoped decision, refresh the task or wave, and continue only already-authorized eligible work. Ordinary authenticated local-app approval is sufficient; cryptographic proof of human presence is explicitly out of scope.

## Required behavior

Reuse `HumanActionCard.tsx`, its query/mutation helpers, the existing `POST /api/gates/<gate-id>/<action>` handler, gate transitions, and continuation callers. The card displays the action, affected task/wave, and limits beside one button. A successful mutation refreshes current state immediately. Errors remain inline with a useful remedy and pending state always settles.

Keep local-session capability authentication, same-origin credentials and origin/CSRF protections, project scoping, current-material CAS checks, duplicate-request protection, ordinary gate audit history, dependency and verification gates, deliberate pause, and existing Start authority. Approval may unblock the authorized wave frontier; it never starts an inert wave, resumes a paused wave, or authorizes siblings or unrelated waves.

Attribute mutations to the configured authenticated actor. UI actions use the local app operator identity; delegated agent calls remain `agent:*`. Never require or claim impersonation as `human:sarav`, and never describe ordinary authenticated approval as verified human presence.

## Removal

Delete the approval-specific signed receipt and signature verification path; native approval-key provisioning, registration, and rotation; approval-only Secure Enclave/Keychain access; challenge issue/expiry/storage; custom Swift/Go signing serialization; both Mac approval bridge handlers; and obsolete tests, configuration, and instructions. Do not retain a compatibility branch, fallback signing path, or backup implementation. Preserve unrelated credentials and shared infrastructure.
