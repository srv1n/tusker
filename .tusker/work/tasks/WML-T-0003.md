## Intent
The daemon runs on its own, started at login, independent of the app window; closing the window keeps work going. Quit Tusker in the menu and tusker stop --all stop the daemon and every process Tusker started, cleanly, using the process inventory. A force-killed app or daemon is cleaned up by the janitor at next start.
