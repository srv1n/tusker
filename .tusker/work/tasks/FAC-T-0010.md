# FAC-T-0010 · Hold the follow-on work when a shared gate comes back red

## Intent

When a shared build-and-test comes back red, the work that was waiting on the
failed piece should be held, not started. Today the follow-on work can still be
picked up even though the thing it depends on just failed, which spreads the
breakage into fresh work copies.

This task holds the follow-on work whenever the piece it depends on fails its
build-and-test, and releases it again once that failure is repaired.

Done looks like: a piece of work whose dependency failed is not handed out and
is shown as held because of an upstream failure, naming the piece that failed;
and once that upstream piece goes green, the held work becomes pickable again.

## Acceptance

| ID | Outcome | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Work whose dependency failed its build-and-test is not handed out and is shown as held for an upstream failure. | Inline verification |
| A2 | The hold names the upstream piece that failed. | Inline verification |
| A3 | When the upstream piece goes green, the held work becomes pickable again. | Inline verification |

## Non-goals

- No cancellation of the failed piece or its dependents; they are held, not discarded.
- No hold across unrelated work; only the failed piece's own dependents are held.
- No change to how a build-and-test decides pass or fail.
- No project-wide freeze; this is a targeted, dependency-scoped hold.
