## Intent

Separate repository-wide V7 tracker hygiene from the autonomous-delivery
critical path without deleting or silently waiving the debt. Inventory the
existing validation findings that were previously attached to AGX-G-0001,
repair their underlying contracts and lifecycle state, and close this task
only when the canonical validators are clean.
