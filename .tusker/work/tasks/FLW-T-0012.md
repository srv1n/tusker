# FLW-T-0012 · Enforce real boundaries without becoming a bureaucratic middleman

## Intent

Publish one rule inventory with owner, rationale, severity and enforcement point. Hard failures are limited to malformed execution contracts, invalid authority, unsafe ownership/state and unmet explicit completion requirements; prose style and advisory quality suggestions remain warnings. Token objective: Lint saves troubleshooting turns and remains scoped; metadata and warnings do not become repetitive prompt payloads.
