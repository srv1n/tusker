# RUN-T-0045 · Configurable disk-pressure guard pauses new dispatch before ENOSPC

## Intent

Yesterday's runner hit `ENOSPC` while Tusker state and worktrees were growing.
The temporary operator advice to keep 10 GB free is not a product policy. Add a
level-triggered disk-pressure guard with configurable byte and percentage floors.
Before creating a workspace, attempt, or runner process, the daemon checks the
filesystems that hold the state root and selected workspace. The effective pause
floor is the larger configured floor. Low space pauses only new dispatch,
reports `disk_pressure` with measured values and paths, and leaves active runs
alone. Dispatch becomes eligible automatically after space recovers.

Defaults must be conservative but machine-independent: 2 GiB and 1% free, with
a warning at twice the effective pause floor. Operators may override or disable
the guard in runtime automation settings. Do not implement retention here.

## Acceptance

|---|---|---|
| A1 | Disk floors are configurable in bytes and percent, default to 2 GiB/1%, and are reported with available bytes, percent, filesystem path, and effective threshold; no 10 GB constant exists. | focused_test |
| A2 | Below the effective floor the daemon creates no workspace, attempt, lease claim, or runner process for new work; active runs continue and invariant state remains healthy. | focused_test |
| A3 | Recovery is level-triggered: after available space rises above the floor, the next poll may dispatch without operator resume; status and Serve expose warning/paused/recovered states honestly. | focused_test |
| A4 | Existing dispatch, capacity, and full-suite behavior remain green. | broad_test |

## Non-goals

- No automatic deletion or retention policy.
- No interruption of active runs.
- No filesystem quota management.
