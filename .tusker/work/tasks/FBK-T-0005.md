# FBK-T-0005 · Lesson promotion: candidate surfacing, canon classes, diversity-preserving pool

## Intent

Feedback notes accumulate under `.tusker/feedback/` but lessons only reach domain canon by hand. Build the promotion pipeline (design sources: docs/design/aie2026-adoption-plan.md workstream 4; docs/design/aie2026-mining/learning-loop.md). Three design decisions from the mining are binding:

1. Recurrence surfaces candidates; a decision promotes them. Detection groups feedback notes by similarity (same friction across notes/repos) and surfaces a promotion candidate at a configurable recurrence threshold (default 3 distinct notes). Candidates never auto-promote — `tusker feedback promote` remains the explicit act, recording provenance: source note paths, repos, recurrence count, and date span.
2. Negative lessons are first-class and typically the highest-value canon (the "avoid X" prohibition class). Canon entries carry a class — `prohibition | pattern | preference` — and packet assembly renders prohibitions in a distinct, prominent section for matching domains.
3. Diversity-preserving pool, not a single champion. Promoting a lesson on a topic does not replace an existing entry on that topic by default; multiple diverse lessons coexist, and superseding an entry requires an explicit `--supersedes <entry>` flag. "Keep only the best" collapses into local optima; the pool keeps distinct winners.

Later, promotion quality gets an eval gate (incumbent-vs-candidate comparison on recorded traces) and per-entry helped/hurt scoring — both need the TRC epic's trace substrate and are out of scope here; this task builds the durable mechanics they will hook into.

## Non-goals

- No eval-gated promotion (incumbent-vs-candidate A/B) and no helped/hurt utility scoring — both follow the TRC trace substrate.
- No automatic promotion under any threshold.
- No cross-vault registry changes (FBK-T-0004 territory).
