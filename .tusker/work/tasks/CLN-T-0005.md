# CLN-T-0005 · Solo profile: collapse human-gate ceremony when operator is the acceptor

## Intent

The close policy forces `required_acceptor: human` for high/critical work, which for a single operator produces closeout packets addressed to yourself — self-review theater. Add an explicit `profile: solo | team` setting in `tusker.yaml`. Under `solo`, when the gate owner and the operating human are the same identity, the human gate collapses to a single needs-me queue entry (surfaced by `tusker next --owner human:<name>` and `tusker brief`) and approval is one command (e.g. `tusker close <ID> --accept`). Under `team`, current behavior is unchanged. Closeout packets remain available on request in both profiles.

Additionally, implement the human-gate taxonomy from CLN-D-0001: gate kinds `clarify` (spec/problem ambiguous), `provision` (human must supply credentials/accounts/resources, with a concrete ask and resume condition), and `approve-spec`. Code review is demoted out of human gates: reviewer_agent may close all risk tiers by default; a human close is required only when the task declares an explicit human gate; tasks may opt into `review: frontier` or `review: human`. `critical` retains named release/security gates.

## Non-goals

- No weakening of `critical` required gates (release/security still apply).
