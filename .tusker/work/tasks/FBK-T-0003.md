# FBK-T-0003 · Collapse duplicate derived feedback signals across repos

## Intent

Promoted from derived-signal analysis. Make multi-vault feedback signals useful by collapsing clone-lane duplicates, grouping causal duplicates, and preserving the distinction between product signals and project-local task hygiene.

## Acceptance

| ID | Outcome | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Multi-vault signal aggregation collapses exact duplicate emissions and records all contributing vaults, sources, and projects as occurrences. | Focused tests cover the three RZN lanes collapsing from repeated emissions to unique grouped findings. |
