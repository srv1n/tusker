# ORC-T-0011 · PM-language morning logbook

## Intent

A `tusker logbook [--date] [--write]` command that composes a daily digest FOR
A PRODUCT READER out of state Tusker already holds. The existing `tusker digest`
(`digestCmd` in `cmd/tusker/v7_escalation_digest_cmd.go`) is terse and
dev/escalation-oriented; the generated dashboards under `.tusker/dashboards/`
are status boards, not narratives. Neither answers a non-engineer's morning
question: what shipped, what does it mean, and what needs me?

Compose the logbook from records that already exist: landed/closed tasks with
their one-line outcomes (from end-state reports / close reasons), gate results
including defect counts and any auto-created repair tasks, open escalations and
human gates, and evidence LINKS (screenshots, diffs, artifacts referenced by
evidence records — link, never inline). Write it in plain product language.

Style contract — write for a PM reading over coffee: lead with what shipped,
what it means, and what needs the human, each with links. No raw command
output. No task-ID-dense prose; IDs appear only as links or footnotes, never
sprinkled through sentences.

## Acceptance

| ID | Outcome | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | `tusker logbook --date <YYYY-MM-DD>` renders that day's digest as markdown to stdout; `--write` persists it to `.tusker/logbook/<date>.md`; with no `--date` the default is today. | Inline verification |
| A2 | The digest is composed only from existing records — closed/landed tasks with their one-line outcome, gate results with defect counts and any auto-created repair tasks, open escalations/human gates, and evidence links — with no re-computation of proof and no external calls. | Inline verification |
| A3 | Output is plain product language: distinct "what shipped / what it means / what needs the human" sections, evidence rendered as links (never inlined), task IDs only as links or footnotes, and no raw command transcripts. | Inline verification |
| A4 | A day with no activity produces a valid, friendly empty digest rather than an error. | Inline verification |

## Non-goals

- No new data capture; the logbook is a projection of records Tusker already
  holds.
- Not a replacement for `tusker digest` (dev/escalation view) or the dashboards
  (status boards); this is the narrative product view alongside them.
- No email/Slack/webhook delivery; it produces the markdown only.
- No inlining of screenshots or diffs; evidence is referenced by link.
