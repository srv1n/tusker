# ORC-T-0001 · Refuse conflicting claims by owned path at claim time

## Intent

On 2026-07-19 (rzn backend), two lanes implemented the same five tasks in one
checkout because claiming is advisory: nothing at claim time checks whether the
paths a lane intends to own intersect another live claim. Plan packets already
carry owned paths. Make the claim step consume them: a claim whose owned paths
intersect a live claim in the same vault is refused with a reason naming the
holder, its task, the lease age, and its liveness (the reason-ful-refusal
pattern from `skills/tusker/references/INTEGRATION_MERGE.md`). Provably-dead
holders follow lease self-heal — liveness probe, auto-release, recorded
takeover — never silent override and never manual lease surgery.

## Acceptance

| ID | Outcome | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | A claim with owned paths disjoint from all live claims succeeds; a claim intersecting a live healthy claim is refused, and the refusal names holder identity, task id, lease age, and liveness verdict. | Inline verification |
| A2 | A claim intersecting a provably-dead holder (failed liveness probe, expired heartbeat) auto-releases the dead lease, records the takeover in events, and proceeds. | Inline verification |
| A3 | The refusal is machine-readable (stable JSON error code + structured fields) and human-readable in the same response. | Inline verification |

## Non-goals

- No cross-vault or machine-global claim registry; scope is one vault.
- No redesign of the owned-path/glob format; consume what packets emit today.
