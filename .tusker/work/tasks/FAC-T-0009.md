# FAC-T-0009 · Cap live worktrees and refuse a gate when disk is too low

## Intent

Building in many parallel work copies at once can fill the disk and wedge the
machine — that is exactly what happened on 2026-07-20. This task puts two
guardrails in place: a hard limit on how many live work copies can exist at
once, and a refusal to start a build-and-test when free disk has fallen below a
floor. Both numbers come from real measurement, never a guess.

Done looks like: trying to open more than the allowed number of live work copies
is refused with a clear reason, and a build-and-test started with too little
free disk is refused up front, telling the operator what to free rather than
crashing partway through.

## Acceptance

| ID | Outcome | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Opening a work copy past the configured live limit is refused with a named reason. | Inline verification |
| A2 | A build-and-test started with free disk below the configured floor is refused, telling the operator what to reclaim. | Inline verification |
| A3 | The limit and the floor come from configured values described as measured, not numbers hard-coded in the tool. | Inline verification |

## Non-goals

- No automatic deletion of work copies or caches to make room; it refuses and tells the operator.
- No rerouting to a remote machine; the escalation-to-VM timer is out of scope here.
- No change to what the build-and-test runs, only whether it is allowed to start.
- No measurement automation; the measured numbers are supplied as configuration.
